Welcome to 56th street market

Located in the heart of Seattle's Green Lake neighborhood, 56th Street Market is your neighborhood market for everyday essentials and quick things you ran out of at home. We offer a wide variety of products, from fresh produce, grocery staples, and household necessities to snacks, treats, and specialty items.
Whether you're stopping by to pick up a few groceries, grab something for the day, or discover something new, we try to make every visit easy and enjoyable. Our welcoming atmosphere, thoughtfully selected products, and friendly service make 56th Street Market more than just a convenience store — we're a neighborhood gathering place.
At 56th Street Market, we're proud to serve our local community and look forward to seeing you soon!
